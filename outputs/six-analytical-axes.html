The Six Analytical Axes

Any organization or product carries a coordinate on all six simultaneously. The paper's analytical work is, in large part, plotting those coordinates and comparing them.

Hero — The Coordinate System

Six independent dimensions, each answering a different question about an organization's relationship to AI. Two organizations plotted as traces: Cursor (an AI supplier with a distribution moat) and a North Italian manufacturing SME deploying Microsoft Copilot.

Second — Axes Are Independent

Stack layer and autonomy level do not predict each other. Cursor and Harvey both occupy the application layer; they enable very different autonomy levels. NVIDIA and OpenAI sit at different stack layers; both enable high-autonomy deployments.

Third — Where the SME Population Clusters

Axes 5 and 6 together reveal the policy challenge: most SMEs sit at the Novice/Taker corner. The target state — Champion-tier adopters operating as Shapers — is reached by only a small fraction. The 40% vs 11.9% large-vs-small adoption gap (OECD 2024) is visible in the density.

Tensions

Independence vs. Correlation

The six axes are analytically independent, but empirically they correlate: infrastructure owners are almost always Makers; interface consumers are almost always Takers. The framework assumes orthogonality; the data may show strong coupling. A firm scoring high on Axis 1 and low on Axis 6 is analytically possible but empirically rare.

Supplier Frame vs. Adopter Frame

Axes 3 and 4 (Archetype, U-curve) apply to AI suppliers; Axis 5 applies to AI buyers. Most real organizations are simultaneously both. A Phase 3 enterprise deploying Cursor is a buyer on Axis 5 and a potential Vertical SaaS on Axis 3. The framework has no native way to handle dual roles.

Axis 4 Depends on H2

The value-capture position axis inherits all the uncertainty of the U-curve hypothesis. If H2 collapses to an L-shape — only the top matters — Axis 4 loses its "bottom of curve" and "squeezed middle" positions. A vocabulary built on a working hypothesis is only as stable as the hypothesis itself.

Archetype Multiplicity

OpenAI is simultaneously a Foundation Model Lab, a Developer Tools provider, and a Consumer AI App. Axis 3 assumes one primary archetype, which is a simplification that obscures the strategic logic of vertical integration — exactly the pattern the paper argues produces durable value capture.
