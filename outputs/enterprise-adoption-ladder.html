Where the value lands in ER&I

Climbing the ladder is necessary but not sufficient. Across the AI value stack, ER&I surplus concentrates at the top (vertical orchestration platforms) and at the bottom (proprietary operational data + physical assets). Generic foundation models in the middle are commoditised inputs to both ends.

What moves a firm up the ladder

Six foundational enablers, in the WEF/Accenture frame. Two industry-level (the substrate), four firm-level (what the company itself owns). The frame's load-bearing claim is that the scaling gap closes once these are in place.

Tensions

Value capture is invisible to the ladder A Phase 4 firm renting a foundation model pays surplus upward. A Phase 2 firm that owns proprietary data may capture more per AI dollar than the Phase 4 firm — even though it scores lower here.
The 74% is interpretation-flexible Sequencing problem, structural ceiling, or no-moat selection effect. The same number reads three ways in the wiki under scaling-gap.
OOD failure compounds at higher phases Deloitte itself is candid that ER&I models fail on novel ores, unfamiliar geology, rare events. Three years of operational trust can be lost in one anomalous incident.
Firm-size dimension is missing from the wiki Every ER&I case here is a large firm — BMW, Aker BP, SBB, Merck, Siemens, Chevron. SME industrial AI adoption needs /discover before the ladder can be re-read with size folded in.
