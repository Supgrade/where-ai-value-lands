# Lee — 01.AI's B2B Pivot

> [!note] Status
> **Stub via synthesis.** This page captures Lee's contribution as braided into [[geopolitics-global-ai-divide]]. The primary materials — Capgemini Research interview (2024) and FT coverage of 01.AI's strategic shift — have not yet been ingested directly.

## Who

Kai-Fu Lee. Founder of Beijing-based 01.AI; previously head of Google China; author of *AI Superpowers*. Among the most articulate Chinese-side strategists of AI value capture.

## Central contributions

**The B2B pivot.** 01.AI walked away from B2C in China because consumer-app dynamics in the Chinese market are winner-takes-all "super-app" wars — platforms must encompass all functions to survive. Instead, 01.AI targets traditional industries: banking, insurance, mining, energy. The logic: bridge China's diffusion deficit ([[diffusion-vs-innovation]]) by embedding AI directly in the gears of the macroeconomy.

**The open-source-for-the-rest thesis.** For almost all countries outside the US and China, building a frontier model from scratch is not feasible. The realistic sovereignty path is taking a leading open-source model and continuing training on local language, values, and regulations. Chinese open weights become the foundation that the Global South builds on — see [[open-weight-asymmetry]].

**The "white-glove service" stance.** Lee argues that AI deployment in traditional industries cannot be self-service. The selling motion is consultative — co-creating applications that generate tangible business outcomes (not merely cost savings) for companies unprepared for digital transformation.

## Why this source matters for the paper

- First-hand evidence of the strategic logic behind China's bottom-heavy value-stack optimum ([[divergent-value-stack-optima]]).
- The Yi-series trajectory (Yi-34B sized for affordable hardware, Yi-Lightning MoE, stockpiled GPUs anticipating embargoes) is a concrete empirical anchor for the [[open-weight-asymmetry]] thesis.
- The B2B "white-glove" framing is a direct rebuttal to the US bet that AI value lands at the API meter.
