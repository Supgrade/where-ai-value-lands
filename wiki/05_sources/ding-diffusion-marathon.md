# Ding — The Diffusion Marathon

> [!note] Status
> **Stub via synthesis.** This page captures Ding's contribution as braided into [[geopolitics-global-ai-divide]]. The primary materials — *Running the Right Artificial Intelligence Race: A National Strategy for AI Diffusion* (RAND, Sept 2025) and *Explaining China's Diffusion Deficit* (FPRI, Sept 2024), plus the *ChinAI* newsletter — have not yet been ingested directly.

## Who

Jeffrey Ding. Assistant professor, George Washington University. *ChinAI* newsletter author. One of the most cited English-language analysts on US-China technology competition.

## Central contribution

**General Purpose Technologies (GPTs) are won by diffusion, not invention.** Historical economic power transitions are rarely decided by the initial breakthrough; they are decided by which nation absorbs the technology fastest across its productive sectors.

See [[diffusion-vs-innovation]] for the concept page.

Two specific applications:
- **The US is training for the wrong event.** Framing AI as a sprint to AGI optimizes for frontier capability while neglecting domestic absorption. Ding advocates a "diffusion marathon" — widening the base of AI engineers, supporting public-private partnerships, helping SMEs adopt AI.
- **China has a diffusion deficit.** Despite massive innovation capacity (patents, publications), institutional bottlenecks and uneven STEM education historically isolated breakthroughs in elite institutions. The current Chinese pivot toward open-weight commoditization and B2B enterprise deployment ([[open-weight-asymmetry]], [[lee-01ai-pivot]]) is a deliberate attempt to close this gap.

## Why this source matters for the paper

- Reframes the US strategic posture as potentially misframed — a much stronger critique than the typical "national security export controls debate".
- Provides the structural explanation for *why* China is bottom-heavy in [[divergent-value-stack-optima]] — not just because of embargoes, but because closing the diffusion deficit is the underlying state imperative.
- Connects to [[task-based-framework]]: if diffusion does not happen, the entire AI productivity surplus may be small enough that no value-stack shape redistributes meaningful gains.
