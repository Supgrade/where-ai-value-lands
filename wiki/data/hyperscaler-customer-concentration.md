# Hyperscaler customer concentration

## The data

> **~70–80% of Microsoft / AWS AI capacity is consumed by just two customers: OpenAI and Anthropic.**

The two foundation-model labs are simultaneously:
- The largest customers of hyperscaler AI compute.
- The recipients of the largest hyperscaler equity investments (MSFT → OpenAI; AWS → Anthropic).
- Building downstream products (ChatGPT apps, Claude Code) that cannibalize their *own* application-layer customers, accelerating those customers' burn.

## Source

Edward Zitron, 2024 reporting, *Better Offline* / wheresyoured.at. Surfaced through [[zitron-circular-economics]] and synthesized in [[bear-case-synthesis]].

## What it shows

A market-structure fact that gives the circular-economy thesis its sharpest edge: the "AI revenue" hyperscalers book on earnings calls is, to a striking degree, money they themselves invested via equity, flowing back as compute spend from two labs. The concentration could be a transient market structure or a permanent feature; the bear case treats it as evidence the demand side is hollow.
