# Perplexity burn ratio

## The data

> **Perplexity reportedly spent ~164% of its revenue on compute (API calls to foundation models) in 2024.**

Comparable points in the same loop:
- OpenAI: ~50% of revenue on inference + ~75% on training (Zitron, 2024).
- Many application-layer startups: **>100% of revenue** flowing to foundation-model API costs.

## Source

Edward Zitron, *Better Offline* / wheresyoured.at, 2024 reporting. Surfaced through [[zitron-circular-economics]] and synthesized in [[bear-case-synthesis]].

## What it shows

A unit-economics inversion at the application layer: revenue does not cover the cost of goods sold (compute). The bear reading: this is structural, propped up by VC funding rather than viable customer economics. The bull reading: a snapshot, with compute costs falling rapidly per unit of capability and enterprise pricing not yet matured.

Note the tension with [[cursor-50b-valuation]] and [[windsurf-acquisition-battle]]: those exhibit the **opposite** signal at the same layer (workflow-embedded distribution moats with healthy ARR multiples). The wiki's interpretation: thin wrappers burn through revenue; workflow-embedded platforms do not. See [[middle-layer-defensibility]].
