# Leader-vs-laggard productivity delta

## The data

> AI leaders (firms in the top tier of the [[enterprise-adoption-ladder]]) report:
> - **4.5% higher cost-efficiency ratios** than peers.
> - **2.4× greater productivity** than peers.
> - **15% revenue outperformance**, projected to **more than double by 2026**.

## Source

Accenture (proprietary survey instruments), cited in World Economic Forum & Accenture, *AI in Action: Beyond Experimentation to Transform Industry*, January 2025. Surfaced through [[wef-ai-in-action-2025]].

## What it shows

The bull-frame quantification of the **leader-laggard gap** that the [[foundational-enablers]] framework is designed to close. The 2.4× productivity claim is the most aggressive of the three and the most exposed to selection bias: Accenture's "leaders" set is sourced from its own advisory client base.

These numbers carry two cautions:
1. **Reverse causality.** Firms that were already operationally excellent are more likely to adopt AI early and well — the AI may be marking, not making, the leader-laggard gap.
2. **Selection bias.** The leader cohort is curated from successful transformations, not a representative sample.
