# Hyperscaler AI capex trajectory

## The data

> **~$800B** in hyperscaler capex over the last 3 years.
> **~$1.7T** planned additional capex for **2026–2027**.

To break even on the cumulative capex, the AI line item would need to generate:
- **~$3T** in net-new AI-specific revenue for break-even.
- **~$6T+** for a normal tech-sector ROI.

For reference: **combined annual revenue of MSFT + META + AMZN + GOOG = ~$1.599T** across *all* products. The required AI revenue line would dwarf the entire combined current business of the four largest tech companies.

## Source

Edward Zitron, *Better Offline* podcast and wheresyoured.at essays, 2024–2025. Surfaced through [[zitron-circular-economics]] and synthesized in [[bear-case-synthesis]].

## What it shows

The financial scale of the capex commitment that the [[circular-ai-economy]] thesis claims is propped up by VC-recycled cash. If correct, the gap between booked capex and any realistic enterprise demand path is the most concrete bear-case fact in the system. If the circular thesis is wrong, the capex still represents a generation-defining bet on AI-specific compute as durable infrastructure.
