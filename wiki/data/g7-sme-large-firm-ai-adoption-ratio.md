# G7 large-firm-to-small-firm AI adoption ratio

Ratio of AI adoption rates between larger and smaller firms within each G7 country, latest available year. Compiled by the OECD from national survey sources (see paper Table A.2). Cross-country comparison must be cautious — firm-size brackets differ across countries.

## What it says

The **EU bloc shows the widest firm-size gaps in AI adoption** (Italy 4.7×, France 3.85×, Germany 2.85×). The Anglo bloc shows the narrowest (US 1.65×, UK 1.85×, Canada 1.9×). Japan sits in between.

This is a striking pattern: countries with the most ambitious SME-targeted *policy* (Italy's Competence Centres, France's IA Booster, Germany's Mittelstand-Digital) also have the largest *outcome* divides. Either (a) policies are too recent to have closed the gap, (b) underlying SME density and firm-size distribution makes the EU divide structurally larger, or (c) policy is failing to reach the bottom of the distribution.

## G7 trend (Figure A.1, % of firms adopting AI)

UK and Germany jumped the most 2024 → 2025 (UK 16.1% → 20.5%; Germany 11.6% → 19.7%). Italy, France, Canada, US all in single digits or low-teens. Japan stable around 18%.
