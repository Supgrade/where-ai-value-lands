# Global AI spend — $632B by 2028

## The data

> **Global AI spend projected at ~$632B by 2028 (29% CAGR, 2024–2028).**
> **GenAI-specific spend: ~$200B by 2028 (59% CAGR).**

Supporting adoption figures from the same paper:
- **65%** of organizations report using genAI in at least one function (McKinsey, 2024).
- **82%** of businesses see genAI as a main lever for reinvention (Accenture).

## Source

IDC (FutureScape forecast series), surfaced through World Economic Forum & Accenture, *AI in Action: Beyond Experimentation to Transform Industry*, January 2025. See [[wef-ai-in-action-2025]].

## What it shows

The bull-frame quantification of the *spend* trajectory — the demand side that the [[hyperscaler-capex-trajectory]] supply side is sized against. The 29% / 59% CAGRs are aggressive by enterprise-software standards but consistent with the period's announced budgets.

The bear question this figure invites: what fraction of the $632B is **net-new** non-tech enterprise demand vs. recycled VC capital and intra-tech spend? IDC does not decompose this.
