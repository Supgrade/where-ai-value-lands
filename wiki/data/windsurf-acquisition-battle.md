# Windsurf — the three-way acquisition battle

## The data

> **Windsurf (Cognition AI):**
> - $82M ARR reached within months of launch.
> - **OpenAI bid $3B** to acquire the company.
> - **Google executed a $2.4B reverse-acquihire** of the human capital (Varun Mohan, Douglas Chen, and core team).
> - **Cognition AI** simultaneously acquired the brand, codebase, enterprise contracts, and developer behavior data.

A three-way bidding contest for the same asset, with each party valuing a different slice (talent / brand+data / strategic neutralization).

## Source

Reporting synthesized in [[where-value-lands-2026]], drawn primarily from Varun Mohan's appearance on Lenny's Podcast and trade-press coverage of the acquisition in 2025.

## What it shows

The most legible market-price reading on what the distribution layer is worth. Three different acquirers, each with a different theory of the case, all converging on multi-billion-dollar valuations for a months-old company at the IDE / developer-distribution layer. The fact that **none** of the bidders were trying to acquire foundation-model capability (they have their own) is the load-bearing observation: the asset that was contested was the human-developer relationship and the behavioral data it produces.

The decomposition of the acquisition (talent → Google, brand+assets+data → Cognition) is itself an analytic artifact: it reveals which slices the market prices independently.

## How to cite
