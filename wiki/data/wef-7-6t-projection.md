# WEF / Accenture $7.6–17.9T projection

## The data

> **$7.6–17.9 trillion** projected contribution to the global economy by **2038** via AI-driven revenue growth.

Supporting line items in the same paper:
- Leading AI adopters report **4.5% higher cost-efficiency ratios** and **2.4× greater productivity** than peers.
- **15% revenue outperformance** by AI leaders, projected to **more than double by 2026**.

## Source

World Economic Forum & Accenture. *AI in Action: Beyond Experimentation to Transform Industry*. January 2025. The $7.6–17.9T range is Accenture's proprietary projection, surfaced in the WEF paper. Surfaced through [[wef-ai-in-action-2025]].

## What it shows

The canonical consultancy-bull number for the size of the AI economic prize. It is **the single bull-case figure most in direct tension with [[task-based-tfp-ceiling]]**: a <0.71% cumulative US TFP gain over a decade is incompatible with a $7.6–17.9T global contribution over the same horizon unless the surplus comes overwhelmingly from new tasks Acemoglu's framework holds fixed.

The figure should be read knowing Accenture has commercial interest in framing AI value as managerially closeable through advisory work.
