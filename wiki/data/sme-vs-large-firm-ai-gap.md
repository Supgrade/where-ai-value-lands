# SME vs Large Firm AI Adoption Gap (OECD 2024)

## Headline figures

| Firm size | Share using AI (OECD, 2024 or latest available) |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------|
| Small (10–49 employees) | **11.9%** |
| Medium (50–249) | **20.4%** |
| Large (250+) | **40.0%** |

**Large-firm adoption is 3.4× small-firm adoption.** Source figure: [[oecd-sme-ai-adoption-2025]], Figure 2, p. 11 (OECD ICT Access and Usage Database, accessed July 2025).

## Cross-country dispersion

The gap holds in **every G7 country** and in every OECD economy with comparable data. Country range observed in Figure 2:

- Korea, Denmark, Sweden, Belgium, Finland: ALL-firm adoption 25–30%, with large-firm rates of 55–70%.
- Germany, Austria, Norway: 20–25% all-firm, large-firm 50–60%.
- **OECD average: 14% all-firm, ~40% large, 20.4% medium, 11.9% small.**
- France: ~10% all-firm, ~30% large.
- Italy, UK, Canada: 6–9% all-firm.
- US, Japan, Australia, Israel: lowest in the OECD ICT survey (3–6% all-firm) — but the US figure understates because the underlying survey is 2021 and the methodology differs (see Annex).

## Comparison to other digital tech

| Technology | Small/large adoption ratio |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Cloud computing | ~1/2 |
| Internet of things | ~1/2 |
| **AI** | **~1/3.4 (less than one-third)** |

The AI gap is materially wider than the gap for any other digital technology — a key data point because it suggests AI has **distinct adoption barriers** beyond the usual size-related digital divide.

## Application-specific gaps (EU 2024, Eurostat)

| Application | Small (10–49) | Large (250+) | Ratio |
|-------------|---------------|--------------|-------|
| Text mining | 5.76% | 21.44% | 3.7× |
| Natural language generation | 4.58% | 16.64% | 3.6× |
| Speech recognition | 3.92% | 16.70% | 4.3× |
| Machine learning (data analysis) | 3.13% | 20.58% | 6.6× |
| Workflow automation / decision support | 3.03% | 20.40% | 6.7× |
| Image recognition / processing | 2.60% | 13.40% | 5.2× |
| **Autonomous robots / vehicles / drones** | **0.66%** | **7.19%** | **10.9×** |

Source: Eurostat 2025, Figure 3 in [[oecd-sme-ai-adoption-2025]], p. 12. **The gap scales with capital intensity** — the more compute / specialized hardware / integration cost an application requires, the larger the SME-vs-large divergence.

## Core-business adoption (G7, 2024)

AI adoption *in core business functions* (i.e., AI directly involved in producing goods or services, not peripheral admin / marketing):

| Country | Core-AI adoption share |
|---------|-----------------------|
| Japan | 1.9% |
| ... | ... |
| United States | 6.1% |

Source: Filippucci et al. (2025), cited in [[oecd-sme-ai-adoption-2025]] p. 9. Full G7 country range: **1.9% to 6.1%**. Roughly an order of magnitude smaller than headline adoption shares because most current AI use is peripheral.

## SME-internal: core vs peripheral

Among SMEs that **do** use generative AI:

- **Only 29% use it in core activities** (OECD 2025 SME survey).
- The majority use it for marketing, content drafting, customer service, internal documentation, research summarization.

## Caveats

- Comparisons across countries should be made with caution — definitions of AI differ, sampling frames differ, and reference years range from 2020 (Israel, UK, Colombia) to 2024 (most EU members).
- The OECD ICT Access and Usage survey has a 10-employee floor — micro-firms (<10 employees), which are the bulk of SMEs by count, are not represented in these aggregate adoption shares.
- "AI use" is a binary survey response. It does not distinguish a Champion-level deployment from a single employee using ChatGPT.
