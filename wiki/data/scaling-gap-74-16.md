# The 74% / 16% scaling gap

## The data

> **74% of companies report challenges in adopting AI at scale.**
> **Only 16% of enterprises are prepared for AI-enabled reinvention.**

Two complementary surveys, published in 2024, surveyed enterprises on AI adoption maturity. The 84-percentage-point gap between investment and value-capture is the single most-cited empirical artifact in the bull-case enterprise literature.

## Source

- BCG Global, October 2024. *"AI Adoption in 2024: 74% of Companies Struggle to Achieve and Scale Value."*
- Accenture, 2024. *"Accelerating reinvention to support growth with AI-powered operations."*
- Surfaced through: [[wef-ai-in-action-2025]] (p. 13).

## What it shows

The gap is **interpretation-flexible** — bull, bear, and middle-dies readings all consume it. WEF/Accenture read it as a managerial sequencing problem solvable by [[foundational-enablers]]. The bear cluster reads it as structural evidence that enterprise AI does not deliver per-dollar. The U-curve reading treats it as enterprise-side evidence of [[middle-layer-defensibility]] — most internal AI initiatives are "thin wrappers" that die for the same reason mid-stack vendors die.

The survey reports the gap but **not its mechanism, its trajectory, or which stack layer the failed initiatives sat on**.

## How to cite
