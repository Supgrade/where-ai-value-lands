# High vs Low AI-Exposure Worker Characteristics

## Data

Per [[massenkoff-mccrory-labor-market-impacts-2026]] Figure 5. US Current Population Survey, Aug–Oct 2022 (immediately before ChatGPT release).

**Comparison groups:** Top quartile of observed-exposure workers (N=32,301) vs zero-exposure workers (N=42,546).

## Key asymmetries

1. **Graduate degree share is ~4× larger** in exposed cohort (17.4% vs 4.5%).
2. **Bachelor's share nearly triples** (37.1% vs 13.3%).
3. **47% wage premium** for exposed work.
4. **Halving of union membership** (5.3% vs 11.7%) — exposed work is overwhelmingly non-unionised white-collar work.
5. **15.5 pp more female** — but conditional on the education profile, not the prior-bear-case "low-education clerical women" framing.
6. **11 pp less Hispanic** — exposure is concentrated in non-Hispanic-white and Asian cohorts.
