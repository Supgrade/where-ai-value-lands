# ER&I use-case productivity deltas

## The data

| Company | Use case | Reported delta |
|---|---|---|
| **SBB** (Swiss Federal Railways) | Computer-vision pantograph inspection ([[predictive-maintenance]]) | **60% inspection-time reduction** |
| **Aker BP** (Norway) | "Yggdrasil" multi-agent offshore operations ([[autonomous-field-operations]]) | **Periodically unmanned platforms** with onshore oversight |
| **BMW** | Multi-agent genAI supply-chain digital twin ([[supply-chain-digital-twin]]) | **30–40% productivity gain** |
| **Merck** | Sequential-learning AI for semiconductor materials ([[ai-materials-science]]) | **€100k+ saved per testing campaign** |
| **Electrical distribution** (sector-wide) | Drone-in-a-box BVLOS inspection ([[autonomous-drone-inspection]]) | **>50% cycle-time reduction** |
| **Siemens** | Energy + waste reduction via predictive AI | Reported reduction (not quantified in source) |

All cases are global rather than US-stack: Switzerland, Norway, Germany. The vertical-industrial value story is **geographically distributed** in a way the software-stack story is not.

## Source

Case studies originate in WEF / Accenture, *AI in Action* (Jan 2025); aggregated and analyzed in the Deloitte AI Dossier for Energy, Resources & Industrials. Surfaced through [[deloitte-ai-dossier-eri]] and [[wef-ai-in-action-2025]].

**Caveat (per [[deloitte-ai-dossier-eri]]):** numerical claims are second-hand. The 60% / 30–40% / >50% figures trace through WEF and would need verification against the originating company reports for paper citation.

## What it shows

The concrete vertical-industrial evidence base for [[vertical-ai-orchestration]]: agentic AI captures value in asset-heavy verticals not by replacing labor wholesale but by orchestrating multimodal sensor → diagnostic → workflow → escalation loops that previously required teams of specialists.

These data points are the empirical anchor of the non-software instantiation of [[middle-layer-defensibility]] — the same workflow-embedded-platform pattern as Cursor, executed against industrial assets instead of code.

## How to cite
