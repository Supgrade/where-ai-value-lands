# Vertical-to-Horizontal Unbundling

The structural thesis (Choudary, 2022 — [[choudary-ecosystem-teardown]]) that drives ecosystem emergence: **as transaction costs fall**, vertically integrated value chains unbundle into **modular, layered ecosystems** where firms at each layer specialise in a single value-creating activity. Horizontal business models then concentrate value at specific layers — the [[ecosystem-business-archetypes]].

## The mechanism

1. **High transaction costs** → vertical integration is efficient. One firm owns multiple stages because coordinating across firms is expensive (Coasean rationale).
2. **Digital infrastructure + new market-organising mechanisms** lower transaction costs.
3. **Unbundling becomes efficient.** The vertical integrator loses its coordination-cost advantage; specialised firms can interoperate at each layer.
4. **Horizontal business models emerge** at each layer (Choudary's Aggregators, Integrators, Infrastructures, Capabilities). Value moves from the vertically integrated incumbent to whoever now organises the *layer*.

## Why it matters for "where AI value lands"

AI accelerates the same dynamic. Two pressures on transaction costs are notable:

- **Foundation models as horizontal substrate** make capabilities that were previously verticalised (translation, summarisation, classification, agentic workflow steps) available as API-purchasable layers. Firms that built these capabilities in-house lose their coordination advantage.
- **Agentic orchestration** further lowers the cost of stitching together specialised services — pushing more activity toward the layered-ecosystem regime.

This sharpens [[H2_u-curve-of-value]]: the U-curve is not an AI-specific phenomenon; it is the **AI-era instance of a recurring pattern** in which falling transaction costs reorganise industries from vertical to horizontal. The squeezed middle exists because once unbundling is complete, value pools at the substrate (Infrastructure / bottom of U) and at the demand-aggregating layer (Aggregator / top of U), with thin coordination layers between.

## Counter-pressures

The unbundling pattern is not monotonic. Two countervailing forces are visible in the AI wave:

- **Re-bundling at the agent layer.** Multi-agent orchestrators ([[vertical-ai-orchestration]]) and "the LLM as operating system" ([[llm-as-operating-system]]) both push toward re-bundling: a single platform coordinates many previously separable capabilities.
- **Geopolitical re-verticalisation.** [[sovereign-ai]] and [[open-weight-asymmetry]] document explicit state efforts to re-verticalise the AI stack along national lines — counter to Choudary's "transaction costs always fall" implicit framing.

Both suggest the unbundling-to-horizontal pattern is a **default tendency**, not a law. It can be reversed by sufficiently strong re-bundling logic (network effects + data flywheels) or by sufficiently strong external coordination (state policy).
