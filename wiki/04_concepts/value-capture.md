# Value Capture

The share of the economic surplus a firm — or a stack position — manages to **keep**, as distinct from the surplus it **generates**. AI vendors plainly create vast aggregate value (faster code, faster customer support, faster materials science); the question this paper exists to answer is *who keeps the resulting margin*. Value creation and value capture are routinely conflated; in the AI stack they pull apart sharply, with whole categories of firm building products that are useful and unprofitable at the same time.

## The creation–capture wedge

The wedge is the central diagnostic of the [[bear-case-synthesis]] and the [[circular-ai-economy]]. Application-layer firms create real consumer surplus while running at deeply inverted margins ([[perplexity-burn-ratio]] at ~164% of revenue on compute); foundation labs create civilisation-scale tools while spending ~50% of revenue on inference and ~75% on training; hyperscalers create the substrate while booking [[hyperscaler-capex-trajectory]] of ~$800B + ~$1.7T planned against revenue lines that may be VC pass-through. Surplus is being generated everywhere along the stack — and the wedge between created and captured value sits inside each layer, not just between them. [[lee-01ai-pivot]]'s B2B re-orientation is essentially an admission that capture in Chinese B2C is structurally hostile to the producer, regardless of the value being created. [[choudary-ecosystem-teardown]] makes the same observation in pre-AI vocabulary: as transaction costs fall, capture concentrates in **archetype positions** (Aggregator, Infrastructure, Integrator switchboard, IP-rich Capability), not in the firms doing the most work.

## Where capture lands — the paper's spatial answer

[[H2_u-curve-of-value]] is the paper's working spatial answer to the *where* question: capture concentrates at the **top** of the stack (workflow + distribution ownership — [[distribution-moat]], [[middle-layer-defensibility]]) and at the **very bottom** (silicon, energy, frontier weights — [[ai-factory-huang]]), with the generic middle squeezed. [[deloitte-ai-dossier-eri]] supplies a non-software instantiation: in ER&I the unit of capture is the multi-agent operational orchestrator ([[vertical-ai-orchestration]]), not the underlying LLM. Each of these claims is a claim about *capture*, not creation — generic middle firms still create value; they just cannot hold it. The labor-side mirror ([[capital-labor-divergence]], [[ai-young-worker-hiring-slowdown]]) extends the same logic: surplus created by AI-augmented knowledge work is captured by capital owners of L4–L6 platforms, not by the workers whose tasks are being automated.
