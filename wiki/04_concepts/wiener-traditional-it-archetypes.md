# Wiener Traditional IT Archetypes

The **pre-AI** baseline taxonomy of IT business models, against which [[weber-ai-startup-business-models]] positions its four AI archetypes. From Wiener et al. (2020), cited extensively in §2.2 of Weber et al.

## The three archetypes

| Archetype | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Data users** | Use big data to streamline their own operations or to create new products/services |
| **Data suppliers** | Collect and sell data to other firms |
| **Data facilitators** | Enable other firms to use big data analytics (infrastructure, analytics-as-a-service) |

Cited Wiener et al. (2020); rooted in Hartmann et al. (2016).

## Why this matters for the project

Weber et al.'s claim is that **AI-charged Product/Service Providers** ([[ai-charged-product-service-provider]]) genuinely **depart** from this triad: data is no longer analyzed to *create insights* — data is used to *train embedded models*. The other three Weber patterns ([[ai-development-facilitator]], [[ai-data-analytics-provider]], [[ai-deep-tech-researcher]]) all map onto extensions of the Wiener triad with ML inside:
- AI Development Facilitator ≈ Data Facilitator + AI tooling.
- Data Analytics Provider ≈ Data User / Data Facilitator with ML inside the analytics.
- Deep Tech Researcher ≈ Data Supplier + frontier R&D in many cases.

This is the load-bearing claim in §5 of [[weber-ai-startup-business-models]]: **only one of the four AI patterns is qualitatively new**; the others extend the pre-AI triad.
