# Audience

## Primary
Founders, VCs, CEOs, and senior operators who **own meaningful assets** (proprietary data, distribution, capital, infrastructure) but **lack a deep technical mental model** of where AI is heading. They need a 24–60 month allocation map and they don't trust the noise.

## Secondary
Technical operators who already understand the stack but want a crisp economic frame to align teams and boards.

## Post-read user job
A reader should be able to:
1. **Place their company** on the agentic AI stack (whatever shape that stack ends up taking in the paper).
2. **Identify which moves are accessible** to them given the assets they already control.
3. **Recognize the traps** — positions that look attractive but get compressed between commoditizing layers above and below.

## What this is *not*
- Not for the general public — assumes business literacy and patience for a long read.
- Not for ML researchers — no novel technical contribution.
- Not neutral. Opinionated synthesis, with skeptic voices included for tension.

## Tone calibration
Peer-to-peer with smart operators. Plain English, no hype, no hedging. Strong claims welcomed *if* they carry a citation or are explicitly labeled as bets.
