# Proposed Structure

~20 pages, English. Theoretical half (scenarios) + practical half (who wins, how), per the kickoff brief.

> [!note]
> Section titles below are working drafts. Names will mutate as hypotheses mutate.

## Sections

1. **Executive summary** *(1pg)* — the central question in one chart, the headline findings, the punch line.
2. **The stack — what an agentic AI system actually is** *(3pg)* — working reference frame for the rest of the paper. Currently scaffolded by [[H1_L0-L7-ladder]].
3. **Where the margin lands** *(4pg)* — the economic question. Currently scaffolded by [[H2_u-curve-of-value]]. Historical analogies (Intel vs. PC OEMs, Stripe vs. acquirers, AWS vs. SaaS, Aggregation Theory) tested, not assumed. Counter-evidence included.
4. **Four scenarios for 2030** *(4pg)* — the theoretical half. Plausible futures with falsifiable signals to watch:
   - *Hyperscaler consolidation* — 3–4 frontier labs + clouds capture the stack.
   - *Open-weight commoditization* — Meta / DeepSeek / Mistral collapse the model moat.
   - *Edge-first fragmentation* — Apple-style on-device wins consumer and mid-market.
   - *Sovereign AI fragmentation* — geopolitics splits the stack along blocs.
5. **Geographic split — US / China / Europe** *(3pg)* — same question, different optima per bloc. Different energy realities, regulatory regimes, capital pools. Currently scaffolded by [[divergent-value-stack-optima]] and [[geopolitics-global-ai-divide]]:
   - **US** — top-heavy optimum: hardware + foundation-model rent extraction; AGI-sprint framing critiqued by [[diffusion-vs-innovation]].
   - **China** — bottom-heavy optimum: open-weight asymmetry ([[open-weight-asymmetry]]) deliberately commoditizes the model layer to push value into B2B integration ([[lee-01ai-pivot]]) and Global-South infrastructure.
   - **Europe** — regulatory-centric optimum: compliance ([[brussels-effect-and-mirage]]) + sovereign infra ([[sovereign-ai]]). Honest treatment of why this currently functions as handicap, not moat.
   - Note: Section 5 may need to function as a **cross-cut of Section 3**, not a standalone — see open question in [[05_open-questions]].
6. **The practical half — who wins, and how** *(4pg)* — per scenario: which positions pay off, what the operator moves look like in practice, what the traps look like. Currently scaffolded by [[H3_orthogonal-axes-under-priced]].
7. **5–10 year visionary horizon** *(1pg)* — energy as the long bottleneck, embodied / robotics, agent-to-agent economy. Speculative, clearly signposted.
8. **Appendix** — link to the LLM-queryable wiki on GitHub, source bibliography, glossary.

## Design notes
- Visually strong infographics for the stack, the value map, the scenario matrix, the geographic split. Strong enough that screenshots get reposted.
- Every strong claim either carries a citation or is explicitly labeled as a bet.
- Each scenario is **falsifiable**: list signals that would confirm or deny it within 24 months.
