# Distribution & Derivatives

## Primary artifact
- ~20pg white paper, English, PDF + web-readable HTML.
- Infographics commissioned alongside the writing, not bolted on after.

## Derivatives planned from day one
- **Public manifesto** (2–4pg op-ed) — Substack / Medium / Sifted / StartupItalia.
- **LinkedIn long-form post + carousel** — infographics extracted.
- **Slide deck** (~15 slides) — VC meetings, board conversations, conference talks.
- **X / Twitter thread** — headline question + central chart.

## LLM-queryable wiki on GitHub
The entire research corpus — tagged sources, hypothesis notes, scenario evidence, the prompts and structure used to assemble the paper — published as a public GitHub repository (license TBD: MIT vs. CC-BY vs. CC-BY-NC, see [[05_open-questions]]) alongside the paper.

Why this matters:
- Lets readers **clone, query through their own LLM, ask follow-up questions, fork it.**
- Signals depth — the paper is the visible tip; the wiki is the iceberg.
- **Compounds after publication.** New evidence can be added without re-publishing the paper.
- Is itself a distribution mechanism — drives technical inbound that a PDF cannot.

## Channels
- **LinkedIn** — primary, given the audience of founders / VCs / CEOs.
- **European pickup** — Sifted, Il Sole 24 Ore Nòva, StartupItalia, TechCrunch EU.
- **US technical reach** — pitch the manifesto (not the paper) to The Information, Stratechery quote-back, Hacker News.
- **Private circulation first** — direct send to ~20–30 named operators / investors / journalists with a personalized note **one week before** public release. Private seeding drives the public launch.

## Timing
- 4–6 week target from kickoff to public release.
- Private circulation begins 1 week before public.
