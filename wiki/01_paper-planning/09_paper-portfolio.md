# Paper Portfolio

> [!abstract] Decision (2026-05-26)
> This wiki now feeds **two papers**, not one. The descriptive paper (P1) and the prescriptive operator-paper (P2) are siblings — different audiences, different questions, same evidence base, same hypothesis surface. Spawning P2 is the consequence of [[H5_ai-as-operational-not-product]], which is written from the operator's seat and surfaces a question the original brief does not answer.

## Background

The original brief ([[00_initial-brief]], [[02_purpose-and-justification]]) framed a single paper aimed at capital-rich, conceptually-light operators. Its question is **descriptive**: *where does economic value land across the agentic AI stack?* The wiki's hypothesis surface (H1–H4) and its concept layer were built to answer that.

[[H5_ai-as-operational-not-product]] (added 2026-05-26) reframes part of the same evidence base from the operator's seat: *given where value lands, what should an operator-builder without a distribution prior actually do?* The descriptive answer to H1–H4 is not a sufficient guide to that question, because the descriptive paper is a map for capital-rich readers who can play any position on the U-curve, and H5's reader is the capital-light operator-builder who can play almost none of them.

Rather than try to braid both audiences into a single paper — which would dilute both — the project now hosts two papers and lets the shared wiki feed both.

## P1 — *Where Value Lands in the Age of Agentic AI*

The existing paper. No change in scope.

- **Question.** Where does economic value accrue across the agentic AI stack over the next 2–5 years (and the 5–10 year horizon)?
- **Audience.** Capital-rich, conceptually-light operators: founders, VCs, CEOs of large enterprises, family-office and sovereign-fund decision-makers (see [[01_audience]]).
- **Stance.** Descriptive inquiry. Opinionated by construction; not a defense of a pre-baked thesis ([[02_purpose-and-justification]]).
- **Hypotheses load-bearing for P1.** [[H1_L0-L7-ladder]], [[H2_u-curve-of-value]], [[H3_orthogonal-axes-under-priced]], [[H4_rl-specialization-value-pocket]]. [[H5_ai-as-operational-not-product]] appears as a *boundary condition* — what the descriptive map looks like from outside the VC-megafund track — but is not the spine.
- **Working title.** *Where Value Lands in the Age of Agentic AI.*
- **Length & shape.** ~20 pages, structure per [[03_structure]].
- **Distribution.** Per [[04_distribution]].

## P2 — *What to Start Now* (working title)

The new paper, founded by H5.

- **Question.** *What should an operator-builder with no distribution prior start now, given where AI value lands?*
- **Audience.** Capital-light operator-builders: solo founders, small partnerships, traditional-industry second-generation operators (the author's home market in particular — Italian / Veneto SME-adjacent), aspiring founders who are reading P1 and concluding that almost none of its positions are reachable from their starting point.
- **Stance.** Prescriptive inquiry. Treats H5 as the founding hypothesis: AI captures durable value as an *operational input*, not as a delivered product, for builders outside the VC-megafund race-in track. P2's job is to make that hypothesis actionable.
- **Hypotheses load-bearing for P2.** [[H5_ai-as-operational-not-product]] is the spine. [[H6_industrial-ai-rollup-captive-suppliers]] (added 2026-05-26 from the same daily-thought reflection that produced the YC five-layer-loop concept) is the rollup variant — H5 at the single-firm scale, H6 at the multi-firm holding-company scale, both Track B. P2 also leans on the buyer-side material the wiki already has — [[non-tech-digital-core-synthesis]], [[wef-ai-in-action-2025]], [[deloitte-ai-dossier-eri]], [[oecd-sme-ai-adoption-2025]], [[fusion-skills]], [[digital-core]], [[taker-shaper-maker]] — and treats them as operator-builder inputs rather than as map features. New operator-side concept pages anchoring P2: [[ai-first-company-loop]] (the five-layer operating model that gives "managed by AI" a concrete architectural definition) and [[software-as-temporary-artefacts]] (the cost-base advantage mechanism that compounds with firm count in H6).
- **Working title.** Provisional: *What to Start Now: An Operator's Map for the Agentic Era.* Italian working subtitle for the Veneto-distributed version: *Cosa avviare adesso.* Revisit after P1 ships.
- **Length & shape.** TBD. Likely shorter than P1 (~15 pages) and more concrete — the deliverable is a decision framework, not a market map. Final structure waits for H5 to harden under at least 2–3 ingests.
- **Distribution.** TBD. Likely a follow-on to P1; same channels but different framing. Italian-language manifesto from [[05_open-questions]] is more naturally a P2 derivative than a P1 derivative.

## The two tracks (the structural reason P1 and P2 are different papers)

| | **P1 reader** | **P2 reader** |
|---|---|---|
| Capital | Has it | Does not have it |
| Distribution prior | Has it | Does not have it |
| Default posture | Choose a position on the U-curve | Build outside the U-curve entirely |
| Useful answer to "what should I do?" | The map | A different question |
| Track on H5 | Track A (VC-megafund race-in) | Track B (operator track) |

The clearest way to think about the split: **P1 is the map; P2 is the playbook for readers who cannot use the map directly.** Both papers depend on the same descriptive findings; they differ in what they ask the reader to do with those findings.

## How the shared wiki feeds both

The wiki is the substrate. Both papers draw on the same hypothesis pages, concept pages, sources, and data. No content is forked.

- **Hypothesis pages** carry a `Used by:` annotation indicating P1, P2, or both, added by `/lint` during the next sweep.
- **Concept pages** are not annotated — the conceptual surface is genuinely shared.
- **Source pages** are not annotated — the evidence is the evidence.
- **Open questions ([[05_open-questions]])** stay shared, with new P2-specific questions tagged `[P2]` in their bullets.
- **Paper structures** live in two separate pages: [[03_structure]] (P1) and a future `03b_structure-p2.md` (P2), to be drafted after H5 has had at least two ingest-cycles' worth of evidence around it.

## Timing and sequence

- **P1 ships first.** Per the original 4–6 week synthesis horizon ([[02_purpose-and-justification]]). The map is the prerequisite for the playbook.
- **P2 drafted after P1 ships.** A 4–6 week interval is the working assumption — long enough to let H5 mature, short enough that the two papers feel like a coordinated release rather than two separate projects.
- **Manifesto and Italian-language derivatives** are P2-flavoured by default; they ride P2's launch, not P1's.

## Open questions promoted to [[05_open-questions]]

- What is the right operator-builder Weber-classifier home? H5 surfaces this gap; see [[weber-taxonomy-2026-gaps]].
- Where does P2 live commercially? P1 is a free PDF + GitHub-Pages wiki ([[04_distribution]], [[06_collaboration]]); P2 might want a different surface — a Substack, a small cohort-based course, an Italian-language landing page. Decide before P1 ships so the P2 surface can be staged.
- Italian-language P2 vs English-only P2: the operator-builder reader the author has most direct empathy for is Italian. Is the natural primary language for P2 Italian (with English secondary), or the same English-primary stance as P1?
