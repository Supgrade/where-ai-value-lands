# Initial Brief

> [!abstract] One-liner
> A secondary-research white paper asking **where economic value lands across the agentic AI stack** over the next 2–5 years, with a 5–10 year visionary horizon, aimed at capital-rich but conceptually-light operators (founders, VCs, CEOs).

> [!warning] Stance
> This brief was written from a kickoff conversation that proposed three working frames: an L0–L7 ladder, a U-curve of value, and a set of under-priced orthogonal axes. **Treat them as hypotheses, not conclusions.** The research may reshape, merge, or retire any of them. The paper is an *inquiry*, not a defense.

## Working title
**Where Value Lands in the Age of Agentic AI**
*An open inquiry into power, margin, and mobility across the AI stack.*

Provisional. Revisit after the first scenario draft.

## Working hypotheses (starting scaffolding)
The kickoff produced three working hypotheses. Each lives in its own note with status, confidence, and "what would retire it" explicit.
- [[H1_L0-L7-ladder]] — a layered map of the agentic AI stack.
- [[H2_u-curve-of-value]] — value concentrates at the extremes of that map.
- [[H3_orthogonal-axes-under-priced]] — the most defensible plays sit off-axis.

These are entry points to the research, not the conclusion. If the evidence kills them, the paper says so — that itself is a contribution.

## Geographic frame
International, English. Differentiated treatment of **US / China / Europe**, especially around energy, regulation, and capital. Europe section written from an honest European perspective — constraints acknowledged, edges named.

## Time horizon
- **2–5 years**: pragmatic, actionable, the operating window.
- **5–10 years**: visionary, signposted as speculative.
