# Source List

Working source map for *Where Value Lands*. Organized by five thematic areas that map to the paper's chapters. Key media channels are listed separately at the bottom — mine them for episodes, don't treat them as standalone sources.

> [!note]
> This is a prioritized shortlist, not an exhaustive bibliography. Goal: enough coverage to calibrate before systematic research begins. See [[02_deep-research-prompts]] for the agent-ready research briefs.

## 1. Vision & AI Trajectory
